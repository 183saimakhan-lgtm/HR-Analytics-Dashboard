# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to explore employee demographics, attrition patterns, salary distribution and employee experience.

The project focuses on exploring HR data, identifying patterns and understanding relationships between different employee attributes.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization

## Data Preparation
Data cleaning and transformation were performed using Power Query.

An Attrition Count measure was also created for employee attrition analysis.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. HR Overview & Attrition
Exploration of employee demographics and attrition-related metrics.

### 2. Salary Analysis
Exploration of average monthly income across different employee categories:
- Education Field
- Age Group
- Department
- Job Role
- Years at Company
- Gender

### 3. Employee Experience & Compensation Patterns
Analysis of relationships between:
- Average Years at Company by Age Group
- Average Job Level by Age Group
- Average Monthly Income by Job Level

## Key Objectives
- Understand employee attrition patterns.
- Explore salary distribution across employee categories.
- Investigate relationships between age, experience and compensation.
- Practise data cleaning and transformation.
- Develop interactive visualizations using Power BI.
